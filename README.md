# Azelficoast

A competitive Gen 9 Random Battle bot built around one rule: **future-you does not get to become psychic.** Azelficoast keeps a belief over what the opponent might actually have, searches under the information the player could really know, and aggressively compiles, caches, and queries whatever it can prove equivalent to Pokémon Showdown. This has somehow resulted in JAX, SQL, a custom compiler, and a concerning amount of database theory in a Pokémon bot. If an optimization cannot carry its receipts, it does not become battle semantics.
